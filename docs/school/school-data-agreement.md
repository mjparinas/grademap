# School data agreement (draft template)

> **Draft for the owner. Not legal advice and not reviewed by a lawyer. Do not sign or send until a lawyer has reviewed it and a real legal entity is named.** Placeholders are in `[brackets]`.

**Between** `[LEGAL_NAME]` ("the Provider") **and** `[School district or school]` ("the School").

## 1. What this covers

The Provider supplies GradeMap, a practice and learning service, to the School's teachers and students. This agreement covers personal information about students that the Provider handles for the School through class accounts that teachers create.

## 2. Roles

The School decides to use the service and which students use it, and is responsible for its own duties under the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act* (BC) `[or the applicable Ontario Act]`, including notice to families and any consent it requires. The Provider handles student information only to provide the service to the School and for no other purpose.

## 3. What information

For each student: a first name or nickname, the class grade and curriculum, an avatar and settings, and practice results. For each teacher: an email address. Nothing else is collected from students: no email, birth year, surname, photo, voice, location or free text.

## 4. What the Provider will not do

- Use student information for advertising, profiling, or to build any product other than the service.
- Sell, rent or share student information, except with the service providers listed in the attached schedule, who are bound to protect it.
- Show advertising or load third-party trackers in the student experience.
- Try to re-identify students from de-identified or aggregated data.

## 5. Where it is stored

The service runs in Montréal, Canada. The schedule lists each service provider and where it handles information. The Provider will tell the School before adding a provider or moving storage outside Canada, and the School may end the agreement if it objects.

## 6. Security

The Provider will keep reasonable safeguards, described in the schedule (encrypted connections, hashed credentials, access limited to the teacher who owns the class, rate-limited sign-in, scrubbed error reports). `[Add independent testing commitments once a penetration test is done.]`

## 7. Breaches

The Provider will notify the School without undue delay, and within `[__]` hours, after confirming a breach that affects its students' information, with what happened, what was involved and what is being done, so the School can meet its own duties. The Provider keeps a record of every breach.

## 8. Access, correction and deletion

The School can see, correct and delete a student's information through the teacher tools, and the Provider will help with any request it cannot complete itself within `[__]` days. A teacher removing a student, closing a class or deleting their account deletes the related student information at once from live systems. After 11 months without a sign-in by the teacher, a linked family or a class student, the Provider warns the teacher by email. After 12 months without a sign-in, the Provider deletes the class, assignments and class-account student information; a parent-linked child is unlinked while their family information is retained by that family account. Backups are removed on the provider's cycle, within `[__]` days.

## 9. End of agreement

On ending, the Provider will delete all of the School's student information within `[__]` days and confirm in writing, unless the law requires otherwise.

## 10. Cost

The classroom tools are `[free for the School at the date of signing / priced as in the attached schedule]`. The Provider will give `[__]` days' notice of any change.

## 11. Contact, law and changes

Privacy contact: `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`. This agreement is governed by the laws of `[British Columbia / Ontario]` and Canada. Changes must be agreed in writing.
